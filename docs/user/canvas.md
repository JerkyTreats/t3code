# Canvas cards

At Comfortable chat width on web and desktop, a thread shows its canvas behind the turns. Any right panel tab can sit on that canvas as a card: a file, Files, a browser tab, a terminal, Diff, a pull request, Linked pull requests, a device or a side chat. A card is the same view the panel shows, with its full header, so you can read a rendered README beside another file's preview above a browser, all at once.

## Put a tab on the canvas

- **From the panel.** Right-click a tab and choose **Place on canvas**. The card lands beside the latest turn.
- **From empty canvas.** Right-click an empty spot to get the same new tabs the panel offers. The card opens with its corner where you clicked. A new terminal, browser tab or side chat starts for the card; a device card opens on the device picker. **Canvas options…** at the bottom opens the canvas's own menu.
- **From a reply.** Right-click a file link in a turn and choose **Preview in canvas**. The card lands beside that turn.
- **From the sidebar.** Right-click any other thread and choose **Place on canvas** to put its chat on the canvas you have open, at a free spot in view. Its card is titled with the thread's name, and only a side chat of the open thread offers **Bring back** and delete there.

## Use a card

Drag a card by its outline to move it, pull a corner to resize it, and delete or undo it like any other shape. Cards stay live at every zoom. A card scrolled far off screen shows only its name until you come back to it, and so does the seventh live card and beyond.

Navigating inside a card changes that card, not the panel: opening a file from a Files card shows the file there, and a new terminal from a terminal card opens as its own card beside it. A browser tab, terminal, side chat or device shows in one place at a time on each screen. While the panel or the floating player shows it, its card says so and offers **Show here**.

A side chat or any other chat placed on the canvas draws on this canvas too. Ask it for a drawing and the drawing appears beside its card. Its drawings move with its card, and a piece you drag away keeps following the card from where you put it.

Each chat has its own colour, shown as a stripe along the top of its card. When an agent draws, its cursor glides in that colour from its chat to the new drawing and briefly outlines it. With reduced motion turned on, drawings simply appear.

Over a browser or device card, the wheel pans the canvas until you click into the page. Everyone in the thread sees the same cards. Two people editing one file in different cards can overwrite each other, and the rendered or raw file view follows your panel setting.

## Cite something on the canvas

Right-click a shape, note or card and choose **Cite** at the top of the menu. The composer gets a citation of it, quoting its text, its label or the card's name, and you can add a comment. Clicking the citation later pans the canvas back to it.
